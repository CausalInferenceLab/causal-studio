Your school district can afford either a tablet for every student or a better library. Another district bought tablets last year. Do their test scores tell you what to buy?

Take four imaginary schools. The two that gave students tablets scored six hundred and seventy-five points on average. The other two averaged five hundred and fifty. That’s a gap of a hundred and twenty-five points in favor of the schools with tablets.

It sounds like a good sales pitch. But before you sign the order, look at who we just compared.
